FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about sending, tracking, and delivering with Parcel Payout.

Still stuck?Ask Copilot, any time.

Create a free account, choose New parcel, and enter the pickup and drop-off details along with the parcel weight. You get a tracking ID as soon as the booking is confirmed.

Enter your tracking ID on the Track page. No account is needed. You will see the current status and the full journey, from pickup through to delivery.

The price is calculated automatically from the parcel weight and any cash-on-delivery amount, and you see it before you confirm the booking.

The courier confirms each drop-off before the parcel is marked as delivered, so there is a record of every handover that you can check from your dashboard.

Raise a claim from the parcel in your dashboard. You can follow the claim there until it is resolved, without phoning anyone.

Sign up and choose Deliver parcels. You can sign in right away, and deliveries unlock once an admin has reviewed and approved your application.
